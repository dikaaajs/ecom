# Product Overview

**Pixel Perfection** is an e-commerce platform specializing in pixel art merchandise and custom designs.

## Core Features
- Browse and purchase pixel art products (keychains, tote bags, stickers, prints)
- Custom pixel art design requests
- Product catalog with filtering and search
- Physical store location integration with maps
- FAQ section for customer support

## Target Audience
- Pixel art enthusiasts
- Gaming community
- Custom merchandise buyers
- Collectors of unique digital art

## Business Model
- Direct sales of pixel art merchandise
- Custom design services
- Both online and physical store presence

## Key Value Propositions
- High-quality pixel art designs
- Exclusive collections (55+ unique designs)
- Custom order capabilities
- International shipping
- Physical store experience
